# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in Task Runner welcoming and
respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
belief.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Listen to different perspectives and accept feedback in good faith.
- Keep discussions focused on the work and the people affected by it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or project work.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
violates this code. To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately at
parloti@gmail.com.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
